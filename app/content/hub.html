Logins are saved as you sign in to sites and filled in for you next time. Everything in the vault is encrypted with AES-256 and never leaves this PC. RECEIVE A SHARED LOGIN

The share text is the login encrypted with AES-256 under the passphrase. Send the text by any channel; without the passphrase it is useless. The other person needs Soot to open it.

CLEAR

Clearing cookies signs you out of websites. Saved logins, favourites and history stay.

IMPORT

From another browser brings over favourites, saved passwords and history from Chrome, Edge, Firefox and others installed on this PC. A favourites file is the .html file any browser can export.

VISUAL EFFECTS

All on by default. Browser effects apply at once. Turn the extra ones off if the browser feels heavy on a slow PC. The new tab page effect follows your Windows "Show animations" setting.
